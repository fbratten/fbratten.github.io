The production story

The documented workflow combines creative direction and human review with AI-generated imagery and video. Production records describe scene prompts, clip generation, manual corrections and selection. The video’s own credits identify the collaborators and tools. Apple Music lists the song on Foreign Sky - EP, released on 19 December 2025, with the label credit ℗ 2025 Adaptivearts Records. Catalogue metadata establishes the release, while the exact released Mixea-master file remains unavailable.

A consistent visual world

The case study follows how the video maintains motifs and characters across generated clips, and where individual scenes needed correction. Selected examples can connect a specific creative decision to the finished frame.

Production, not a single render

The story includes orchestration through SPINE, work in NeuralFrames and Kling, music development in Suno and subsequent audio tools. Exact roles should follow the final credits rather than treating the full pipeline as automatic.

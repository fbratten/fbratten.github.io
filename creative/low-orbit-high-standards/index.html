The production story

Earlier MUZAK work develops a male baritone narrator and a female Mission Control voice. The production story centres on their different attitudes and on making interruptions feel immediate rather than separated by an unwanted pause.

Write the voices as roles

The narrator’s ceremonial confidence and Mission Control’s procedural delivery create the scene. Instructions before the sung lines establish the roles without turning them into explanatory comments inside the lyric.

Timing carries the punchline

The selected process example will connect an interrupted declaration to Mission Control’s response. The selected Studio export’s text preserves the interrupted declaration followed by “Hold”, and ends with “And what about those fantasies?”. It contains no role markers. That confirms the words and their order; the intended female voice and removal of the pause still require listening to the chosen audio.

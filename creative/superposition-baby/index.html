“Weights in the file, features in the flow”

A compact hook, with a useful distinction.

Weights are stored model parameters. Activations are values computed for an input. Interpretability research studies recurring patterns in those activations as features. The weights shape how those patterns arise; the hook simplifies that relationship.

“More concepts than you got cells”

Superposition as a musical image.

In the cited toy-model research, a network can represent more features than it has dimensions by using overlapping directions. Applying this picture to larger models requires care; a feature is not simply one neuron with one fixed meaning.

Why does the first verse get it wrong?

The disagreement is the teaching device.

The song first presents a misconception, then challenges it. The exchange gives the listener both the misconception and the answer, using rhythm and repetition to make the distinction memorable.

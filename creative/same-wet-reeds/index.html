The production story

An earlier version, Same Wet Reed (Heri Fugit, Orig Ed), contains the arrangement instructions for fingerpicked acoustic guitar, sparse đàn bầu replies, an unhurried 6/8 sway and no drums or percussion. Those written intentions belong to that version; the public Studio export linked here has a different ID and still needs a matched listening check.

An instrument as an answer

In that earlier arrangement, the đàn bầu is asked to answer the vocal phrases rather than simply double them. The artistic question is how a small reply can change the weight of the preceding line.

One text, different approaches

The saved September 13 history contains 19 different style metadata entries; their lyrics/direction fields were not saved, and some styles mention brushed drums. They remain separate from the later no-drums instructions. An earlier annotated version, a longer concatenated version and the public Studio export are three distinct records; their exact lineage remains unproved.

The production story

The documented pilot explored three storyboard concepts. The Jacket Relay became the working direction, developed through Fredrik’s choices, ChatGPT’s artistic coordination and a concept contribution from Gemini’s Argon.

A motif that connects the scenes

A jacket becomes a recurring visual element. The process makes continuity a creative question: what carries forward from one scene, character or moment to the next?

Show the stage the work has reached

The ledger reports 40 storyboard scenes ready in Audove. The video-generation stage was inspected but not started in that documented run. This case therefore presents visual development rather than a finished music video.

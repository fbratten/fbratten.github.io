Music / Film production story

Studio – New York – New York

By Fredrik Brattén · Artist name: Ragnar Bratten

A singer, a photographer and the work of an atelier: an 80-second film shaped around one continuous passage of the song.

Explore the production ↓All works

Human direction, model-specific tasks and a carefully assembled edit.

Direct the scene

Fredrik’s direction keeps the action grounded in the atelier: the singer performs, the photographer works with the camera, and small gestures connect the clothes, light and people in the room.

One opening revision is especially concrete. The direction keeps the photographer present through the shot while the woman turns her head slightly and brings a hand toward her temple in response to the light. This becomes a precise motion task, followed by a separate lip-sync pass.

ChatGPT, agents
& Fal

Fredrik steered the creative work and reviewed the results. ChatGPT coordinated the tool-assisted production: prepare shot instructions, request model runs through Fal, review takes, assemble the edit and check the export.

Give each model a bounded job

Kling 4.0
Image-to-video motion for the atelier, camera work and other cutaways.
Kling Avatar v2 Pro
Performance takes driven by short audio guides from the song.
Sync Lipsync
Version 2 Pro and version 3 were run during refinement of the opening performance.

The cooperation moves through concrete handoffs: a shot brief becomes a generated take; a selected take and its audio guide become a lip-sync task; the reviewed clips become inputs to the edit.

Keep the music continuous

The film uses the song’s 03:16–04:36 passage as one continuous 80-second audio bed. Visual cuts follow the music while performance takes use the corresponding phrases as guides. The finished presentation runs at 24 frames per second in 1280 × 720.

Methods in practice

Fredrik chose two of the methods developed in this ecosystem for the production: Dialogue Lifecycle and 5PP Variation A.

Dialogue Lifecycle: move from an idea to a reviewed result

The work moves through the visual idea, clarifying the desired action, choosing a direction, generating and editing, then reviewing the result. Feedback on the opening sends that shot back through another revision.

5PP: make the task and its checks concrete

The scope fixes an 80-second film and a continuous musical passage. The production plan divides motion, performance, lip-sync and editing into distinct tasks. Export checks examine duration, frame timing, audio continuity and alignment.

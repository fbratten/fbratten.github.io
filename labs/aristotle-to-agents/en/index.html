How to read the map

This is a source-oriented teaching model. Chronology does not itself establish cause. An analytic analogy compares phenomena. A risk arrow describes a possible mechanism without a measured probability. Controls may reduce risk without guaranteeing truth.

The five argument categories form a modern thematic grouping. Human fallacies, model generation errors and prompt injection have different mechanisms. Rhetoric can be used well, and an incorrect model answer does not imply intent.

The 74 nodes and 111 relationships come from the material dated 1 November 2026. The original grounding labels are shown as inherited assessments. Selected primary sources were checked on 3 November; this is not a new verification of every historical relationship. The fifth view and the five experiments were added on 3 November.

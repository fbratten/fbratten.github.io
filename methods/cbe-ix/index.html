CBE
Capability Boundary Expander / Iterative Exhaustion

Follow the gaps.
Know why you stopped.

CBE-IX repeats bounded capability analysis over the updated target. It stops when no further legitimate target-owned expansion remains under the available evidence, or when a distinct blocking condition prevents valid progress.

CBE plus explicit convergence

Each accepted change can reveal another gap

The base CBE classifications still apply. CBE-IX adds a frontier of unprocessed target-owned questions, an iteration ledger, duplicate detection and an identity check after every material change.

What may re-enter the frontier?

  • A KEEP responsibility that exposes a new completeness question.
  • A target-owned part of a SPLIT.
  • An unresolved target area newly supported by evidence.

Delegated operations, rejected candidates, semantic duplicates and already exhausted responsibilities stay out. A peer requires its own separately scoped analysis.

What stays in the ledger?

Input target signature, frontier before and after, gaps, candidate verdicts, accepted delta, handoffs, unresolved items, material progress and convergence status.

Invariant: expansion must preserve the target's original responsibility. Rewording an old responsibility is not progress.

expand(Tn) = Tn describes a semantic fixed point: another valid pass adds no new target-owned responsibility or unprocessed target-owned SPLIT fragment. Textual equality alone is insufficient.

Seven checks before claiming exhaustion

  1. C1: the unprocessed target frontier is empty.
  2. C2: a further scan finds no new target-owned gaps.
  3. C3: there is no new KEEP delta.
  4. C4: target-owned SPLIT fragments have been processed.
  5. C5: nothing still unresolved can be resolved with currently available evidence.
  6. C6: the target identity remains intact.
  7. C7: no semantic cycle is present.

A hard resource limit is reported as an external limit. An unresolved architectural ownership decision that makes further expansion risk peer takeover is reported as a boundary blocker. Neither is relabeled as successful exhaustion. Unavailable evidence that leaves no further valid expansion path may instead remain explicit residual uncertainty.

          Eight distinct terminal states

          EXHAUSTED_VALID
          Fixed point reached, identity preserved, verification passes and no material unresolved boundary remains.
          EXHAUSTED_WITH_HANDOFFS
          Target expansion is exhausted; required adjacent operations are explicitly delegated.
          EXHAUSTED_WITH_RESIDUAL_UNCERTAINTY
          Available bounded alternatives are exhausted, but unavailable evidence or an external decision leaves residual uncertainty.
          BLOCKED_BY_BOUNDARY_DECISION
          Further expansion depends on an unresolved architectural ownership decision; proceeding would risk taking over a peer responsibility.
          TARGET_IDENTITY_DRIFT
          The identity check after a material accepted change finds that the design no longer preserves the original target. One change or several accumulated additions can trigger this stop; the relevant acceptance decisions must be reconsidered.
          INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION
          The invariant or current responsibility set cannot be established well enough to iterate.
          NON_CONVERGENT
          Semantic cycles or repeated equivalent candidates prevent valid convergence.
          EXTERNAL_ITERATION_LIMIT_REACHED
          The environment ends the run before semantic exhaustion. Preserve the frontier for re-entry.

          Residual uncertainty describes the exhaustion of available bounded alternatives, not complete knowledge. New authoritative evidence can justify re-entry with the previous ledger preserved.

          Use the smallest sufficient stack

          Start with Capability-Gap and CSR when existence or coverage is unclear. Use SORR to resolve ambiguous ownership. Apply CBE-IX when repeated internal expansion is justified; use CBE for a bounded analysis without the exhaustion requirement.

          Dialogue Lifecycle governs transitions and 5PP Variation A governs scoped work and its audit. A CBE-IX terminal state records the analysis outcome. A subsequent implementation still needs its own accepted scope and authority.

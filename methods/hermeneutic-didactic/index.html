Hermeneutic-didactic

Interpretation that develops understanding, and interpretation that turns a recorded run into a teachable, governed artifact

This profile compares two related but non-equivalent implementations. SPINE DIALECTIC uses model-backed iterative interpretation, scratchpad rehydration, synthesis checks and RigVedan findings. ChangePlane performs a deterministic whole-parts-reinterpreted-whole pass over first-class run records, derives one lesson, emits a derived didactic projection and drafts a memory-admission candidate.

10
Evaluation interpretation and boundaries
The mechanisms make interpretation and teaching more inspectable. Their real effects require separate measurement.
Problem definition
AI work often loses how understanding evolved and what future work should learn from a completed run. A result may survive while the interpretive path, unresolved questions and teachable lesson disappear.
Mechanism hypothesis
Iterative horizon accumulation should make evolving interpretation inspectable, while deterministic whole-parts rereading plus governed projection should make run-derived lessons more reproducible and auditable. These are hypotheses.
Possible evaluation
Measure correction rate, unresolved-question recall, reviewer agreement, lesson source coverage, unsupported-memory admission, future-run rework, time to reorientation and resistance to misleading convergence signals.
Failure modes
Model loops can converge around a wrong reading. Deterministic rereading can be stable but shallow. Selected parts can omit decisive evidence. Didactic output can oversimplify, and candidates can be promoted without adequate review outside these mechanisms.
Explicit non-claims
  • The implementations are not equivalent and are not merged into one protocol.
  • SPINE similarity, synthesis and completion signals do not prove truth.
  • ChangePlane fixpoint convergence does not prove semantic completeness.
  • A didactic projection is derived and cannot outrank its source records.
  • A memory candidate is not a durable-memory write.
  • A tamper-evident seal does not prove the original reasoning was truthful.
  • Committed tests do not establish real-world learning or decision quality.
  • No independent comparative user study was identified.

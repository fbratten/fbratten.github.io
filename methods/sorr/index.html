SORR

Semantic Orientation & Relationship Resolver reduces semantic misorientation across distributed systems: identity, representation, relationships, evidence, scope, time and claim authority remain separate until the evidence supports a bounded resolution.

Problem

The same underlying subject can appear through repository names, local copies, branches, cards, projections, indexes and runtime observations. SORR prevents labels, references or newer observations from being silently upgraded into identity or authority verdicts.

Core model

observations
-> semantic references
-> bounded resolution
-> qualifications + support + residual uncertainty
-> downstream orientation / action

Key boundaries

reference != identity verdict
representation != represented subject
provenance != authority
resolution != projection
resolution != execution
UNRESOLVED != FAILURE

Composition

SORR composes with ORBIT for orientation, CARD/POINTER for bounded representation and routing, and other systems for observation, coordination or execution. Transport never becomes semantic ownership merely by carrying a result.

Authority and scope

The governed engineering sources and semantic contracts remain the deeper authority. This page is a public-safe projection of SORR as a capability family and does not turn it into a universal truth engine or transfer semantic ownership to adjacent systems.

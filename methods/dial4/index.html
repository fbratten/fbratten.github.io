DIAL-4

Keep evidence, implication, utility and bounded validity from collapsing into one undifferentiated claim

DIAL-4 separates four reasoning modes: Deduction, Inference, Abatement and Legitimation. The framework asks what the source supports, what it suggests, what burden it may reduce, and what remains valid after scope control and verification. ChangePlane implements a related claim-record surface, but its six recommended claim types are not a one-to-one encoding of the four conceptual modes.

01

Four modes, four burdens

Each mode asks a different question and protects against a different category error.

Evidence

Deduction

What can be responsibly concluded from the source? Direct support is required.

Meaning

Inference

What does the source reasonably suggest or imply beyond its direct findings?

Utility

Abatement

What burden, cost, dependency, friction or uncertainty may be reduced?

Bounded validity

Legitimation

What survives explicit scope control, method discipline and verification?

    11
    Scientific interpretation and boundaries
    The framework is best understood as claim hygiene and didactic separation, not an automatic truth engine.
    Problem definition
    Technical writing and AI output often place direct findings, implications, business benefits and bounded verdicts in the same paragraph at uniform confidence. The reader cannot tell what kind of claim is being made.
    Mechanism hypothesis
    Explicit claim typing, strength grading and scope-aware legitimation should make category errors, hidden inferential jumps and utility-as-proof easier to detect. This is a design hypothesis.
    Possible evaluation
    Measure inter-rater agreement on claim types, unsupported-deduction rate, inference-to-fact conversion, utility-overclaim rate, caveat survival, scope leakage, reviewer correction time and downstream decision quality.
    Failure modes
    Labels can become decorative, categories can be assigned inconsistently, a legitimation pass can merely restate conclusions, confidence can be uncalibrated, and a record schema can create a false impression of rigor.
    Explicit non-claims
    • A DIAL-4 label does not prove that a claim is true.
    • Deduction in this framework is evidence-bound claim handling, not a formal-logic proof system.
    • Inference does not become deduction because confidence is high.
    • Abatement does not prove feasibility, maturity or return on investment.
    • Legitimation does not add evidence that was absent from the source.
    • DIAL-4+ confidence is not automatically calibrated.
    • ChangePlane's claim-type vocabulary is free-form and not a closed semantic validator.
    • The two DIAL-4P meanings are not interchangeable.
    • Committed tests do not establish real-world reasoning outcomes.
    • No independent annotation-reliability or efficacy study was identified.

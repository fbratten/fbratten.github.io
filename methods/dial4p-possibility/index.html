DIAL-4P Possibility

Preserve viable possibility space, structure uncertainty, and turn “could be” into bounded proof and implementation paths

DIAL-4P Possibility composes four layers. DIAL-4 separates claim types. DIAL-4+ grades strength through confidence, evidence quality and scope. Diátaxis routes the knowledge into a suitable document form. 5PP governs what happens next through clarification, scope, planning, execution and verification.

01

Governing rule

Possibility is preserved unless evidence, contradiction, explicit constraints, incoherence, impossible premises or a hard non-negotiable limitation force collapse.

Preserve

Do not confuse absence with impossibility

Lack of packaging, standardization, vendor support or current evidence does not by itself justify rejection.

Structure

Classify and divide

Uncertainty receives an explicit status, operational divisions, dependencies, proof paths and falsifiers.

Constrain

Possibility is not permission to overclaim

A possibility remains bounded by source authority, scope, verification and the strongest supported claim mode.

Anti-collapse is not anti-rejection.Rejection remains valid when a hard condition is stated and evidenced. The method rejects premature collapse, not disciplined pruning.

11
Scientific framing and non-claims
The profile treats DIAL-4P Possibility as a documented method and reusable prompt system. Outcome effects remain open research questions.

    

      
Problem definition
Binary evaluation often collapses unbuilt, unstandardized or unevidenced ideas into “not possible,” while optimistic analysis can preserve everything without creating accountable next steps.

      
Mechanism hypothesis
Typed claims, strength grading, documentation routing, status classification and operational division should reduce premature rejection and unsupported advancement while producing more actionable proof paths.

      
Possible evaluation
Measure false rejection, unsupported advancement, status stability, time to first proof, falsifier quality, division completeness, reviewer agreement and eventual implementation yield.

      
Failure modes
Possibility debt, endless deferral, optimistic relabeling, vague proof paths, status inflation, hidden hard constraints, tool-use ceremony and document-form overengineering.

    

    
Explicit non-claims
    
      
  • Every unresolved idea should not be preserved indefinitely.
    • 
      
  • Lack of evidence is not evidence of feasibility.
    • 
      
  • Classification does not make a claim true.
    • 
      
  • Operational division does not guarantee implementation success.
    • 
      
  • Required memory, tool use and 5PP adherence are not mechanically enforced by prompt text alone.
    • 
      
  • The nine-status list and six-status article subset are not silently treated as identical contracts.
    • 
      
  • Diátaxis is an external documentation framework used in this first-party composition.
    • 
      
  • DIAL-4P Possibility is not ChangePlane Projection/Productization.
    • 
      
  • No independent outcome or inter-rater study was identified.

Public-safe, source-aware profiles

The systems behind the systems

Profiles of the protocols, methods, grammars, semantic mechanisms and capability families that underpin the technical project estate. Public profiles are bounded projections: they distinguish current maturity, authority, implementation evidence and non-claims rather than treating publication as proof of runtime completion.

Catalog reconciled against the governed method/capability landscape in September 2026. Some deeper authorities are private. Those profiles expose only public-safe orientation and explicitly preserve the boundary between public projection and internal source authority.

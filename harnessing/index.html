First, the idea

What is a harness?

The surrounding system that connects a model to context, tools, working rules, limits and feedback.

Harnessing is the work of choosing, connecting and checking those parts for a particular task.

A skill can be one part. A memory store, review protocol or execution gate can be another. Their value depends on the problem and how they are actually used.

The building blocks / What connects to what?

From a method to a working pipeline.

A useful harness combines different kinds of support. The method guides a part of the work; an actor uses it at a particular moment; workflows turn the work into repeatable units; a pipeline connects those units through explicit handoffs.

PartIts responsibilityAt a freight terminal
ModelLanguage and interpretation capabilities used within the working system.Interprets a driver's update and helps formulate alternatives.
MethodA way to approach a specific kind of problem.DIALECTIC helps revise the explanation when the new arrival time conflicts with the original plan.
SkillA reusable procedure for carrying out a bounded task.A booking-check procedure specifies what to inspect and what result to return.
AgentAn actor working on a task using its available context, tools and authority.The planning agent prepares options; the authorized operating actor decides the live assignment.
WorkflowA repeatable unit with inputs, activities, outputs and checks.The exception-analysis workflow turns a timing mismatch into conditional options.
PipelineConnected workflows whose outputs satisfy the next workflow's input needs.Arrival monitoring, exception analysis, booking coordination and verification form one delivery process.
HarnessThe surrounding context, working rules, tools, state and checks that support the actor's task.The agent has relevant sources, a scope boundary, a handoff path, stop controls and evidence for the final status.

Select the support that the situation calls for. A simple lookup may need only a clear source route; a changed operational plan may also need interpretation, coordination and a verified decision.

Anthropic Research13 June 2025

Research with explicit delegation and retained state

Anthropic describes a lead researcher delegating bounded work to subagents, synthesizing returns and using a citation step. Its engineering account also discusses retries, checkpoints and resumption after failures.

What this helps explain. It illustrates how a research pipeline needs coordination, bounded effort and continuity around the model calls.

What does each contributor return, and what state lets the lead continue after an interruption?

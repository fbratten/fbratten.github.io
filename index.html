  • Pin source and implementation receipts.
  • Separate tests, runtime evidence and documentation claims.
  • Use synthetic data in public demonstrations.
  • Keep limitations beside strengths.
  • Route risky actions through policy or human approval.
  • Preserve earlier evidence when systems evolve.
